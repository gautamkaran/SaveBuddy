# Who Is SaveBuddy For?

SaveBuddy is a simple tool, but "save a link fast, keep it private" turns out to be useful in a lot of situations. Here are the most common ways people actually use it.

## Developers

The web is a stream of docs, Stack Overflow answers, API references, and GitHub repository readmes. SaveBuddy is useful for:

- Keeping a working **reference links** list per project.
- Saving **docs pages** you hit every week - no more re-Googling the same thing.
- Stashing **example repos** to review after feature work.
- Keeping code snippets, tools, and libraries in a quick-access list.

The `Alt+S` shortcut makes it nearly instant: highlight nothing, just press the keys and keep typing.

## Students

Research throws dozens of tabs into the browser. SaveBuddy helps by:

- Saving the **sources** you used for a paper or assignment.
- Keeping **study links** (practice problems, reference charts) in one fast list.
- Working **offline** - links stay local even on campus networks that block cloud tools.
- Not needing an account on a shared computer.

## Researchers

For researchers, privacy is the selling point:

- Save **sensitive topics** without anyone's server logging your history.
- Annotate by editing titles: `Lit - Kwon 2023`, `Data - Census 2020`.
- Keep reading lists local on the institution machine.

## Recruiters and hiring managers

- Collect candidate **profiles and CVs** in a private list - no HR SaaS required.
- Track **job boards and postings** with one-click save-tab.
- Keep everything off company auditing servers if you prefer.

## Privacy-conscious users

If you dislike the amount of tracking in the modern web, SaveBuddy is refreshing:

- **No account**, no email, no password.
- **No analytics** - there's no telemetry in the extension at all.
- **No cloud** - your link list never leaves your computer.
- Fully **open source**, so the privacy claims are verifiable.

## Minimalists

Some people just want a link keeper, not a "productivity ecosystem." SaveBuddy stays small: a popup, five buttons, one list. No dashboards, no quotas, no upgrade banners, no notifications.

## Everyday users

- Save **recipes** and shopping lists you found while browsing.
- Keep **watch-later** videos in one place.
- Stash **gift ideas** as the holiday shopping season approaches.
- Park **articles** you'll read on your own machine later.

## What SaveBuddy is not for

Honest limits, so you know upfront:

- **Multi-device sync** - links stay on one computer by design.
- **Full-page clipping** - SaveBuddy stores the link, not the page content.
- **Heavy organization** - there are no folders, tags, or full-text search.
- **Reading view** - that's Pocket's job, not SaveBuddy's.

## Start with a use case

The tool installs in about 30 seconds, so the fastest way to find your use case is to just use it:

1. [Install SaveBuddy](/installation/)
2. Go to a page you like, hit **SAVE TAB**.
3. Come back to it tomorrow with `Alt+S`.
